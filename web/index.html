Changing the brain starts a new conversation. The same model can play both roles.

How it works

Brain (Bielik 1.5B in vLLM or Gemma 4 E4B in Ollama) reads the command, thinks and decides what to use. It has two tools: turn_camera turns the head (Wemos D1 mini + 2× SG90), and describe_view takes a photo and asks the eyes for a description.

Eyes (Gemma 3 12B or Gemma 4 E4B) look at the photo and describe it in Polish. The brain gets this description as the tool result and answers based on it.

On the right you can see every step in order: the brain's reasoning (expandable), tool calls, the photo the eyes received, their description and the final answer.
